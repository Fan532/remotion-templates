# EP10 dependencies, assets and rights review

Existing pinned stack: Remotion/@remotion/cli4.0.538, React19.3.1, TypeScript7.0.2, systemChromium, FFmpeg and Noto Sans CJK SC. No new video runtime dependency or online paid-generation service.

Reused from the user's own repository: EP04/EP08/EP09 bilingual typography, card/easing helpers, caption system, original Owner SVG character and local audio/verification tooling. Copies are isolated in EP10 and do not overwrite their originals.

New original artwork: bakery interior/storefront, loaves/croissants/baguettes, shadows/glass, shelving, bags, cash register, coffee machine, clock and steam. No third-party images, footage, movie clips, stock 3D meshes, character models or sampled music. Required 3D assets were not present as a verified authorized asset pack; the deliverable uses explicit layered 2.5D SVG/CSS, not a claim of a complete 3D character pipeline.

Noto Sans CJK is SIL Open Font License. The system renders glyphs; font source files are not redistributed. Remotion itself uses a separate Free/Company license, available in node_modules/remotion/LICENSE.md; check eligibility for the organization before commercial deployment. The package ISC field does not supersede Remotion licensing.

Music and gentle cues are generated by the episode's own sine/harmonic synthesis scripts using generic chords, with no samples or third-party melody. Human narration is the user's supplied recording and is retained at its original speed. Local SenseVoice alignment is production tooling, not voice synthesis and not a runtime video dependency.
